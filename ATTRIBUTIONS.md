# Asset credits

The Macfolio shell and portfolio implementation are original. The four integrated applications are vendored from Hamid Shaikhy's [Barg](https://github.com/hamidshaikhy/barg-resume-builder), [Hamnava](https://github.com/hamidshaikhy/hamnava), [Personal Finance](https://github.com/hamidshaikhy/personal-finance) and [To Do List](https://github.com/hamidshaikhy/to-do-list) repositories, with exact commits in `integrations/sources.json` and compatibility patches in `integrations/patches/`. Their source documentation and supplied dependency/license credits are retained. Personal Finance screenshots come from its pinned repository and show demonstration data. The root Barg and To Do List SVGs use their original app marks; Hamnava's music-note and Finance's dollar/banknote Dock SVGs are original generic drawings requested for this integration. The upstream PWA icons remain unchanged. Visual inspiration for the shell: [ThanasOS](https://thanasos.thanas.dev/) and [playground-macos](https://portfolio.zxh.me/).

The interactive About Hamid and standalone portfolio reconstructs the composition and interactions of [Talha Badar's portfolio](https://talhabadar.com/) with Hamid's information, verified projects and original implementation. The reference's source code and media are not distributed here. The aligned natural and electronic upper-body portraits were generated from Hamid's supplied photograph using the built-in image_gen tool, at his request. The approved electronic portrait includes stylized OpenAI / ChatGPT / Codex, Claude and Cursor marks on microchips; those marks belong to their respective owners. His supplied handwritten signature was cleaned and recolored using the same tool. Original PNGs and exact prompts are preserved in `output/imagegen/`; optimized transparent WebP versions are served locally. The small two-tone JavaScript, TypeScript and Next.js footer SVGs are original implementation-specific renderings of the respective brand marks; all trademarks belong to their owners.
